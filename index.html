How to Play:
Start by tapping on the screen to generate a pair of colored blocks at the top of the grid.
Swipe left or right to move the blocks horizontally, and swipe down to speed up their descent.
Double-tap on the screen to rotate the blocks.
Align the blocks with others of the same color to create and clear lines.
Be careful not to let the blocks reach the top of the grid, or it's game over!
