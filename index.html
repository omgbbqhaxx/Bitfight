The arsenal

Four colors. Four powers.

Every block you pop does something. Pick the right color at the right moment and the match is yours.

Attack

Pop red blocks to hit your rival. Every red you clear takes that much off their health.

Heal

Clear green to win back health and stay in the fight a little longer.

Boost

Yellow grants a temporary edge — extra attack or extra defense when you need it most.

Disrupt

Blast blue to rain a burst of random blocks onto your rival's board.

How to play

Easy to learn.
Hard to put down.

Blocks fall from the top. Line up same-colored blocks, then drop a killer block on them: it destroys every connected block of its color and scores a point for each one.

+

The bigger the chain, the bigger the payoff.

  1. Swipe left or right

    Steer the falling blocks into place.

  2. Swipe down

    Speed up the drop when you know where it goes.

  3. Double tap

    Rotate the pair to lay blocks side by side.
